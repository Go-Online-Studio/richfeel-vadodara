### The Science Behind Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT)

The core science behind Photo Bio Therapy lies in advanced Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT). By exposing the scalp to specific, calibrated wavelengths of light, this Advanced Scalp Therapy aggressively stimulates the cellular metabolism within the hair roots:
- **Increased ATP Synthesis**: When light energy penetrates the scalp tissue, cellular mitochondria produce more ATP, the primary cellular fuel.
- **Enhanced Micro-Circulation**: Local capillary dilation delivers oxygen, minerals, and growth building blocks straight to the dermal papilla.
- **Reversal of Miniaturization**: Resting follicles are gently yet powerfully pushed back into the active anagen growth phase.
