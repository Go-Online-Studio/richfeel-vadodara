### Understanding Biotin-Enriched PRP

Platelet-Rich Plasma concentrates your body's platelets to release growth factors. At Richfeel, PRP is uniquely enriched with micronized **Biotin**, providing the fundamental building block for keratin synthesis.

### The Next-Gen Leap: GFC (Growth Factor Concentrate)

GFC represents an engineered evolution. Platelets are activated in high-precision vacuum tubes to release pure PDGF, VEGF, EGF, and IGF-1 without RBC or WBC contamination. This extends the active anagen growth phase and significantly delays catagen follicle regression.
