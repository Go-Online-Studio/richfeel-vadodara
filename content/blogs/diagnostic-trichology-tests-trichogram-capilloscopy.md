### Beyond the Naked Eye

Most generic clinics prescribe shampoos or pills based purely on visual inspection. At Richfeel, diagnosis begins with scientific optical measurements.

### The 3 Core Diagnostic Tests

1. **Trichogram**: Measures the exact Anagen to Telogen (A/T ratio), establishing whether hair shed is seasonal or progressive androgenic miniaturization.
2. **Capilloscopy**: High-resolution video-dermatoscopy analyzing scalp micro-circulation, sebum accumulation, and follicular ostia health.
3. **Follicular Count Test**: Accurately counts follicle density per square centimeter to monitor therapeutic progression over time.
