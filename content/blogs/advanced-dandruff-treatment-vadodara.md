### Differentiating Dandruff Types for Targeted Relief

Dandruff is not a single, uniform condition, which is exactly why generic drugstore treatments consistently fail:
1. **Sticky Sebum Dandruff**: Caused by hyperactive sebaceous glands, forming thick yellowish scales that suffocate hair roots.
2. **Fungal Malassezia Overgrowth**: Yeast proliferation that triggers intense itching, erythema, and rapid epidermal shedding.
3. **Chronic Flaking**: Dry, brittle flakes aggravated by environmental pollutants and harsh chemical hair products.

Our certified trichologists formulate an individualized treatment plan utilizing ultrasound cleansing and anti-inflammatory botanical complexes. This restores scalp micro-ecology and stops the vicious cycle of chronic shedding.
