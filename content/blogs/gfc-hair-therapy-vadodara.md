### The Science of GFC: An Advanced Hair Fall Treatment

GFC is a highly concentrated preparation extracted from your own blood, rich in specific growth factors that are vital for tissue regeneration and follicle repair. When injected into the scalp, it acts as an intensive **Hair Root Strengthening Therapy**. This Advanced Scalp Therapy directly targets dormant hair follicles, pushing them into the active growth phase (anagen). By doing so, it serves as an unparalleled **Hair Follicle Strengthening Treatment**, significantly reducing hair shedding and promoting new, resilient strands. 

For those seeking a reliable **Hair Fall Control Treatment**, GFC stands out because it is 100% natural and autologous, meaning there is zero risk of allergic reactions. ### The Step-by-Step GFC Procedure

When you visit our clinic, your journey begins with a comprehensive microscopic scalp analysis:
1. **Blood Collection**: A small, standard sample of your blood is drawn in a completely sterile environment.
2. **GFC Extraction**: Using specialized, advanced centrifuge technology, we isolate the high-concentration growth factors from your blood cells. This step is crucial for creating a potent Hair Root Strengthening Therapy.
3. **Precision Application**: The concentrated growth factors are then meticulously administered to the targeted areas of the scalp experiencing thinning.

The entire session takes under an hour, making this Natural Hair Fall Control Treatment a perfect "lunchtime" procedure with zero downtime.
