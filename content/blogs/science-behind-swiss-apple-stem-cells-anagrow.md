### What is Anagrow?

Richfeel Anagrow is an internationally developed hair rejuvenation treatment born from collaboration with **Dr. Fred Zulli (Mibelle Group, Switzerland)** and **Dr. Ferrari (Biotic Labs, Italy)**. It targets the 5 primary signs of progressive hair thinning:

1. Scanty hair patches
2. Decreased overall volume
3. Delayed follicle regrowth
4. Declining follicle density
5. Chronic diffuse hair loss

### The Breakthrough Formula: PCT Rejuve MAX

The formulation relies on rare botanical stem cell extracts of the Swiss Uttwiler Spätlauber apple, Moroccan Argan tree sprouts, and Pea sprout peptides. Combined with Nano-Lipobelle Co-enzyme Q10, these microscopic liposomes penetrate the scalp barrier.

### Transdermal RF Anagain 10X Delivery

Unlike painful injections, the RF Anagain 10X machine uses non-invasive transdermal radio-frequency waves to deliver vital phytonutrients deep into the dermal papilla. This triggers cellular rejuvenation and stimulates dormant follicles back into the active anagen growth phase.
