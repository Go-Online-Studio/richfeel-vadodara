### The Hidden Cost of Hair Coloring

Chemical hair dyes, bleaching agents, and ammonia disrupt the lipid mantle and strip the hair cortex of natural moisture. This leads to dry split ends, scalp burning, and accelerated hair shedding.

### Power THP from the USA

Richfeel introduced **Ana-d-tox** featuring Power THP formulation containing 3 hydrolyzed plant proteins:

- **Quinoa Proteins**: Smooths outer cuticle scales and locks in pigments to prevent premature fading.
- **Barley Proteins**: Restores deep cellular moisture to the scalp barrier and reduces frizz.
- **Wheat Proteins**: Calms chemical scalp irritation while reinforcing tensile shaft strength.

Clinical trials demonstrate a **20% increase in color retention** and **25% enhanced color uptake**.
